Delete your SIPLX account

You can do it here, on this page, without installing anything. It removes your account and its contents straight away, and it cannot be undone.

Delete it here

Step 1 — prove it is your account

Sign in with the same Google account you use for SIPLX. We show you exactly what will be deleted before anything happens, and you can still stop.

Choosing this loads Google’s sign-in code from accounts.google.com, at that moment and not before. We receive an identifier for your Google account, never your Google password.

Choose your account in the button above.

Step 2 — what will happen

Also delete the snippets I left with my teams. Leave this unticked to let those teams keep using them.

Or delete it in the app

Open SIPLX → Settings → Account → Delete account

The same thing, by the same route: it asks you to sign in once more, shows you what will happen, and then does it.

If the button above will not work for you

The page signs you in with Google because that is how SIPLX accounts sign in. If yours does not — an older account from before September 2026, a Microsoft work account, or a Google sign-in your browser or network blocks — write to privacy@siplx.app from the address on the account. We delete it by hand, with the same outcome as below, and confirm when it is done.

What happens

Deleted immediately

  • Your email address, display name and profile picture
  • Your sign-in details: the link to your Google account, and a password if your account ever had one
  • Your snippets and their text, including their version history
  • Your trigger phrases and folders
  • Everything synced between your devices
  • Your membership of every team
  • All active sign-ins, on every device

Kept

  • Snippets you chose to leave with a team. They stay in that team and remain usable by its members. Leaving them is the default; the tick box above deletes them instead.
  • Teams you own, which pass to another member — see below.
  • One anonymised record, for 30 days. Your row in our database survives with everything identifying stripped out — no email, no name, no sign-in details — and is removed after 30 days.

Why one record stays for 30 days. Content that other people still use — snippets you left with a team — has to belong to something in the database, and it is attached to that empty record rather than to you. And because the address is not released immediately, nobody else can take it the moment you leave. The record holds no personal information, cannot be signed in to, and is removed 30 days after you delete the account.

If you own a team

Ownership moves to someone else so the team keeps working:

  1. The member you nominated as successor, if you named one and they are still in the team.
  2. Otherwise, whoever joined earliest.
  3. If you are the only member left, the team is dissolved and its snippets are deleted with it.

Step 2 above names the person each of your teams will go to, before anything happens.

Never created an account?

If you have only ever used SIPLX without signing in, there is nothing here to delete — your snippets are on your device and never reached us. Uninstalling the app removes them.

删除你的 SIPLX 账号

在这一页就能删，不用安装任何东西。账号和里面的内容会立刻删除，无法撤销。

在这一页删

第一步：点上面的「Continue with Google」，用你平时登录 SIPLX 的那个 Google 账号登录，证明是你本人。

第二步：页面会先列出会发生什么——多少片段会被删、你拥有的团队会交给谁——这时还可以取消。确认之后才真正删除。

也可以在应用里删

打开 SIPLX → 设置 → 账号 → 删除账号

如果上面的按钮对你不管用

这一页用 Google 登录，因为 SIPLX 账号就是这么登录的。如果你的账号不是——2026 年 9 月之前的老账号、Microsoft 工作账号，或者你的浏览器、网络挡掉了 Google 登录——请用账号上的那个邮箱地址写信到 privacy@siplx.app。我们手工删除，结果和下面写的一样，删完会回复你。

会发生什么

立即删除：你的邮箱、显示名称、头像；登录信息（与 Google 账号的关联，以及账号曾经设过的密码）；你的片段及其文字和历史版本；触发词和文件夹；各设备之间同步的全部内容；你在所有团队里的成员身份；所有设备上的登录状态。

会保留：你选择留给团队的片段——它们留在团队里，成员可以继续使用（默认保留，上面的勾选框可以改成一并删除）；你拥有的团队会交给其他成员：优先给你指定的继任者（仍在团队内的话），否则给最早加入的成员，如果只剩你一个人，团队解散、片段随之删除；另外会留下一条匿名记录，保留 30 天后删除——里面没有邮箱、名字和任何登录信息，无法登录，只是为了让留给团队的内容有归属、并且你的邮箱不会马上被别人占用。

从没创建过账号？

如果你一直没登录就在用 SIPLX，那这里没有什么可删的——片段只存在你的设备上，从未传到我们这里。卸载应用即清除。
