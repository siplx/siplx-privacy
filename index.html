SIPLX Privacy Policy

What SIPLX stores, what leaves your device, and how to remove it.

Last Updated: 12 August 2026

SIPLX is a text-expansion app. Snippets you write are saved on your device and work without an account. Two features send data off your device, and both are optional: AI formatting, which you trigger one snippet at a time, and cloud sync, which uploads your snippet library continuously once you sign in and turn it on.

If you do not sign in, nothing is uploaded by cloud sync. SIPLX is fully usable as a guest: your snippets, folders and triggers stay on your device and are never sent to us.

1. What we collect

Account information

Collected only if you create an account.

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • An account identifier we generate
  • Either a password (stored only as a cryptographic hash, never in readable form) or, if you sign in with Google, an identifier Google gives us for your account. We do not receive or store your Google password.

Snippet data — uploaded only when cloud sync is on

When you sign in and enable cloud sync, the following is uploaded:

  • Snippet titles and body text — everything you have written in a snippet
  • Trigger phrases — the shortcuts you type to expand a snippet
  • Folder names and folder codes
  • Related details: snippet type, expansion mode, created and modified times, how many times a snippet has been used and when it was last used, and whether it is marked favourite, archived or deleted

Attachments are never uploaded. Images, PDFs and any other files you attach to a snippet stay on the device you added them to. They are not sent to our servers and do not appear on your other devices. If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone, attachments are not restored — use Export to keep your own copy.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photo library, or files outside what you attach in the app.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not sell data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not record what you type in other apps. Expansion happens on your device.

2. Where your data is stored

Cloud sync data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon, in Amazon Web Services' Asia Pacific (Singapore) region. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Neon processes this data on our behalf as a hosting provider. It is not sold or shared with anyone for their own purposes.

3. AI formatting

When you tap the AI formatting button on a snippet, the text of that snippet is sent to our AI gateway and forwarded to a third-party language-model provider (OpenRouter) to produce the formatted result. This happens only when you explicitly ask for it — never automatically, and never for snippets you have not chosen.

The snippet text is used to generate your result and is not stored by us as part of this feature. Your account credentials are not sent to the model provider.

4. Team workspaces

If you join or create a team, content you place in that team's workspace is shared with the other members of that team.

  • Every member of a team can see and sync the full content of team snippets — body text, trigger phrases and folder — regardless of their role.
  • Team members see your display name. They do not see your email address. Email addresses are not sent to other members' devices.
  • Please note that your display name may be derived from other information: if you registered without entering a name, it is the part of your email address before the @; if you signed in with Google, it is the name on your Google profile. You can change your display name to anything you are comfortable sharing.
  • Content already synced to another member's device cannot be recalled. Please do not put passwords, keys or private information in team snippets.

5. Deleting your data

Individual snippets

Delete a snippet in the app and it is removed from your device and, if cloud sync is on, from your cloud library on the next sync.

Your whole account

Open Settings → Profile → Delete account in the app. You will be asked to confirm who you are first — with your password if you registered with an email address, or by signing in with Google again if your account uses Google. This works for both kinds of account.

You can also delete your account from the web, without installing the app: siplx.github.io/siplx-privacy/delete-account/.

When you delete your account:

Your personal snippets, triggers and folders in the cloudDeleted
All active sign-in sessionsRevoked immediately
Your email address, display name and passwordRemoved from your account record
Snippets on your deviceStay on your device — deleting your account does not erase your phone
Team content you chose to leave with the teamKept for the team, with your identity removed from it

When you delete your account you are asked whether to also delete the snippets you contributed to teams. If you choose to leave them, they remain available to those teams and are no longer associated with you.

After deletion, a record of the account remains in our database in a de-identified form — without your email address, display name or password — so that the account cannot be signed into or re-registered. We are working towards removing these records entirely on a schedule and will update this policy when that is in place.

6. How long we keep things

  • Cloud sync data is kept while your account exists, so it can be delivered to your devices.
  • Deleting a snippet or your account removes that content as described above.
  • Sign-in sessions expire on their own and are revoked when you sign out or delete your account.

7. Children

SIPLX is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Your choices

  • Use SIPLX without an account. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Sign in but leave cloud sync off. Your snippets stay on your device.
  • Use cloud sync without AI formatting, or the reverse. They are independent.
  • Export your snippets at any time from Import & Export, as CSV or JSON.
  • Delete your account from the app or from the web page linked above.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change what SIPLX collects or how it is used, we will update this page and change the "Last Updated" date at the top. Material changes to how we handle data that has already been collected will be announced in the app.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, can be sent to siplx.app@gmail.com.
